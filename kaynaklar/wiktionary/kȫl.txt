===Etymology===
According to Bonmann and Fries et al. (2025), {{bor+|trk-pro|qfa-yen-pro|*xuɬ|t=water|nocap=1}}<ref>{{cite-journal|last=Bonmann|first=Svenja|last2=Fries|first2=Simon|year=2025|title=Linguistic Evidence Suggests That Xiōng-nú and Huns Spoke the Same Paleo-Siberian Language|url=https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1111/1467-968X.12321|journal=Transactions of the Philological Society|volume=0|pages=1-24|doi=10.1111/1467-968X.12321|page=9|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20250812093208/https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1111/1467-968X.12321|archivedate=12 August 2025}}</ref> via {{w|Arin language|Old Arin}} {{l|qfa-yen-pro|*xuɬ|*kul}}, around the time of the {{w|Xiongnu|Xiōngnú Confederacy}}.

According to Doerfer (1967), perhaps {{bor+|trk-pro|pal|𐫐𐫇𐫓|t=pit, sink|ts=kōl|sc=Mani|nocap=1}}, whence {{cog|fa|کول|tr=kôl|t=pit, lake}}.<ref>{{R:TMN|vol=III|page=645}}</ref> This is supported by Monshizadeh.<ref>{{R:fa:Monchi-Zadeh:1990|page=109}}</ref> Nişanyan deems the Middle Persian word a cognate.<ref>{{R:tr:Nişanyan|göl}}</ref>
