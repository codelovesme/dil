====Reconstruction notes====
Nişanyan and Doerfer postulates that the {{w|Proto-Turkic}} word comes from an earlier '''*tuwuz (*tubuŕ)''', explaining the aberrant forms of {{cog|cv|тӑвар}} and {{cog|mn|давс}}, which is a loanword from [[Turkic]].
