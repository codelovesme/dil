==Proto-Turkic==

===Etymology===
Şemseddin Sâmi proposes a derivation from {{suffix|trk-pro|*āg-|*-r|alt2=*ïr|gloss1=to rise}}, whence {{cog|tr|ağmak|t=to descend; rise, move up}}. However loss of long vowel is problematic. See potential cognates {{m|trk-pro|*agrïg||pain, ache}}, dialectal {{cog|tr|ağdık||oblique; heavy}}.
