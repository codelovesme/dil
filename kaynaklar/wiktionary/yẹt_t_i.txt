===Reconstruction===
It remains unclear whether ''*t'' was geminated or not. Although most reflexes show a simple ''*t'', the sporadic distribution of ''*tt'' suggests that {{m|trk-pro||*yẹtti}} was the original pronunciation, sustained by Clauson (1972),<ref>{{R:trk:Clauson|entry=yétti:|page=886}}</ref> and that the geminates were later simplified in each branch independently.
