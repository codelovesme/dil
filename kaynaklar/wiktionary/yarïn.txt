==Proto-Turkic==

===Etymology===
Unclear whether derived from {{m|trk-pro|*yaru-|t=to shine}} or {{m|trk-pro|*yarï-|t=to shine}}{{suffix|trk-pro||*-n}}. But Old Turkic having yarïn rather than expected *yarun.

Gagauz points to a long vowel.<ref>{{R:gag:trBaskakov|page=254}}</ref> Therefore {{w|Talât Tekin}} (1995) reconstructs it as {{m|trk-pro|*yārïn}} and compare it to {{cog|tyv|даарта}}.<ref>{{R:trk:Tekin1995|page=63}}</ref>
