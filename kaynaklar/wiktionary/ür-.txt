==Proto-Turkic==

===Etymology===
Nişanyan suggests a derivation from earlier {{m|und|*üwür-}} or {{m|und|*üyür-}}, a derivation from the onomatopoeic {{m|und|*ü}} or {{m|und|*üw|t=blowing sound}}, which he and Räsänen compare to {{cog|tr|üfürmek|t=to blow}} and {{cog|az|üfürmək|t=to blow}}, both from {{cog|trk-oat|اورمك|tr=üvürmek}}.<ref>{{R:tr:Nişanyan|ürümek}}</ref>. An onomatopoeic origin from would also make this root related to {{cog|tr,tk|üflemek}}, {{cog|az|üfləmək}}, {{cog|gag|üflemää}} etc.. Räsänen also compares {{m|trk-pro|*ör-|t=to speak loudly}}<ref>{{R:trk:Räsänen|page=522}}</ref>
