==Proto-Turkic==

===Etymology===
{{ety|trk-pro|:af|*ïra-<aftype:root><t:to be far>|*-k}}
Derived from {{m|trk-pro|*ïra-||to be far, draw away}} + {{m|trk-pro|*-k|pos=deverbal adjective suffix}}.<ref>{{R:trk:Clauson|entry=ırā:k|page=214}}</ref> The initial {{IPAchar|/j-/}} found in certain descendants (e.g. Karakhanid, Kipchak) is secondary and prosthetic, as shown by the absence of {{IPAchar|/j-/}} in Old Turkic and the Oghuz and Siberian branches.<ref>{{R:trk:Clauson|entry=ırā:k|page=214}}</ref>
