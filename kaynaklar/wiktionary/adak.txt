==Proto-Turkic==

===Etymology===
Usually considered to be [[simplex]], however some theories have been proposed with ultimately no conclusive solution to the derivation of this term, these include:
# Németh and some other linguists derive it from {{m|trk-pro|*āt-|t=to take a step}}.
# Nişanyan suggests a derivation from {{m|trk-pro|*ad-|t=to separate}}, however states that the morphology is not very obvious as there is no deverbal noun forming ''*-ak'' suffix.
# Hamilton puts forth the possibility of being derived from {{m|trk-pro|*yād-|t=to spread}}.<ref>{{R:oui:Hamilton:2020|page=144}}</ref>

Sevortyan's attempt at relating it with {{noncog|ine-pro|*h₁ey-|t=to go}} and Räsänen's attempt to link it with {{noncog|ko|바닥}} are unfounded.
