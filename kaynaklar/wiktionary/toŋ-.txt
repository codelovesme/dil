===Etymology===
According to Wilkens, {{bor+|nocap=1|trk-pro|ltc|凍|tr=tuwng, tuwng{{sup|H}}|t=to freeze; ice, cold}}.‌<ref>{{R:oui:Wilkens|page=731|entry=toŋ-}}</ref>
Compared by Altaicists to {{noncog|xgn-pro|*daxara-|t=to freeze}} and {{noncog|tuw-pro|*doŋota|t=cold, frost}} ({{noncog|evn|доӈото}}); Räsänen compares {{noncog|fi|tankea}}, from {{noncog|urj-fpr-pro|*taŋketa}}. However, the Altaic hypothesis is widely discredited and these words aren't related genetically.
