====Reconstruction notes====
In Bulgar, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, and Southern Altai (noninitial ''-e'' is assimilated to ''-ö'' by labial vowel harmony rules), the final vowel ends in ''-ü'' (vowel harmony in the Kazakh letter {{m|kk|у}}, however, is unreflected) instead of ''-i'', as other Turkic languages do.
